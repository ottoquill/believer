## Why not deeper

Both reviews say "overwritten," and the structural latitude was a license to *subtract*. But the recon showed the book's repetition is mostly the **intentional chorus** — the "stop knowing" insight recurs ~12 times, but largely in *distinct voices* (Tamsin's feature, the Quiet Room ad, the Department memo, Anton, Daniel's public speech, the forum). That distribution is the idea *propagating through the culture*, and gutting it would remove a feature, not a flaw. The genuinely trimmable cases were the **narrated** restatements, which I targeted.

So this pass removes ~300 words of true redundancy plus the bug/simile fixes, rather than slashing 3,000 words of distinct, high-quality prose. If a more aggressive 3–5% reduction is wanted, the next targets would be: a second room compressed out of ch11 (it holds three), trimming ch22's Anton prescription, and condensing ch15's tents-meditation — but each means cutting good, distinct material, so I'd want a deliberate go-ahead.
