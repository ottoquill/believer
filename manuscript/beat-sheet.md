# PART I — THE WINCE  (Days −1 to 21)

### ch01 — "The Save"  · N · 3,400w
- **Purpose:** Establish Daniel's genius and amorality at his peak; the voice; the world; dramatic irony for everything to come. The reader must be a little seduced by how *good* he is at lying.
- **Beats:** Day −1. Daniel runs a televised crisis press conference for **Governor (client)** caught in a scandal. Show his method — not crude lying but *reframing*, feeding the governor lines, managing the room, the press, the optics. A masterstroke turns the story. Champagne after with Gideon Vance ("There are no facts, only the story nobody's bothered to beat yet"). Glimpse home: a missed dinner, a text from Nadia, June asleep. Seed Priya: a single uneasy detail Daniel waves off (a name in an email, a buried file) — *he believes it's nothing.*
- **Open on:** the instant before the governor speaks — Daniel's hand on his shoulder, the whole machine humming.
- **Close on:** the win complete; Daniel alone, briefly, a hairline of something he can't name; foreshadow without explaining.
- **Plant:** Priya/Halcombe (lightly); Gideon's creed; the family at the edges; phones (he lives on his).

### ch02 — "Kitchen"  · N · 3,000w
- **Purpose:** Onset. The condition arrives and detonates the marriage in the same scene, before Daniel understands anything.
- **Beats:** Day 1, morning. Ordinary domestic friction. Daniel tells a reflexive small lie (to June or to Nadia — "I'll be home tonight," or "it was nothing") and is lanced (calibrate: trivial→sting, but the *next*, bigger reflex lie surges). Confused, frightened. In the same kitchen, he asks Nadia an ordinary question; the field makes her answer with the truth of **her secret** (the colleague, the year of wanting). She doesn't know why she said it; he doesn't know why he can't. Two people gut-punched by a force neither names.
- **Open on:** a domestic still-life that the chapter will demolish.
- **Close on:** the silence after the truth; June in the doorway; the chasm.
- **Plant:** Nadia's secret now live; June present; the merciful-lie thesis (Nadia, defensive, articulates it for the first time).

### intA — "Narrative Strategy: Q3 Reputation Architecture"  · I · 700w
- **Form:** A leaked VMA internal slide deck / strategy memo (pre-onset), satirizing the managed-lie trade. Bullet-points, euphemism, "managed disclosure," "narrative beats," "stakeholder sentiment." Funny and chilling. The world Daniel built. Contains, buried in a footnote, the **Priya Raman containment** line item rendered in bloodless corporate prose.
- **Purpose:** Satire + the buried sin in the record, deniably.

### ch03 — "Experiments"  · N · 3,000w
- **Purpose:** Map Rules 1 & 2 through dread, not delight (the inverted "discovering my powers" beat).
- **Beats:** Days 2–7. Daniel, alone, tests: small lies vs. big (pain scales); phone vs. in person (**the field doesn't carry through phones — he can lie freely on a call, agony in the room**); writing is safe; silence is safe but legible. Discovers the *field* when a delivery driver, a doorman, a stranger in an elevator can't lie near him. The horror of an always-on jurisdiction. He starts managing his life around it (hallways for calls, written notes).
- **Open on:** Daniel in a mirror, trying to lie to his own face.
- **Close on:** the elevator stranger's confession; Daniel understands it's *the room.*
- **Plant:** phones-as-sacrament motif established hard; the edge-of-room behavior.

### ch04 — "The Meeting"  · N · 3,000w
- **Purpose:** Career implosion; the field as professional poison.
- **Beats:** Days ~10. A client war-room — the thing Daniel is best at — becomes a massacre. The client, lawyers, Gideon: none can sustain the lies the work *requires.* Strategies collapse into involuntary candor; someone admits the actual crime; nosebleed-grade pain for the big lies. Gideon realizes Daniel is radioactive — a fixer near whom no one can spin is the end of the business. Daniel eased out / suspended "for his health."
- **Close on:** Gideon, almost tender, cutting him loose: a man who deals in stories has become an inconvenient fact.

### ch05 — "Necessary Fictions"  · N · 3,200w
- **Purpose:** The intimate cost; the marriage cannot hold; June in a no-lie house.
- **Beats:** Days 14–21. Home life under the field. Nadia and Daniel cannot perform the small mercies a marriage runs on. June asks questions and gets, for the first time, only true answers (a small devastating exchange — a dead pet, a "do you and mom still love each other"). Nadia's thesis sharpens: *"I tell the truth all day. Mercy is the part they pay me for."* Daniel realizes the condition is permanent and total; he cannot fix this with words, his only tool.
- **Close on:** acceptance; the door of his old life closing; the scale of the new one.
- **Pay/plant:** June's catechism established; Nadia thesis deepened.

### intB — "Session 14"  · I · 900w
- **Form:** A couples-therapy session transcript, set in the new world, between two ordinary people who can no longer lie to each other — comedy curdling into grief. The therapist's own helplessness (she relied on kind reframings). 
- **Purpose:** Theme (society runs on small mercies) at population scale; satire + ache.

# PART II — PROXIMITY  (Weeks 4–12)

### ch06 — "The Loophole"  · N · 3,200w
- **Purpose:** Discover the belief loophole as **horror**, the book's dark engine.
- **Beats:** Daniel notices anomalies: a salesman lies near him and *doesn't flinch*; a conspiracy-believer asserts nonsense painlessly; a scrupulous, honest acquaintance who says "I think, but I'm not sure" suffers more than the serene crank. Daniel works it out: **pain tracks knowing-falsehood, not falsehood.** The honest bleed; the self-deceived go free. He grasps the monstrous implication for a world that learns this. (And — unstated, dramatic-ironic — it's why *his* belief about Priya doesn't hurt him.)
- **Close on:** Daniel realizing the safest people in his presence are the ones most certain of things that aren't true.
- **Plant:** the engine of Crane; Daniel's own shielded self-deception (reader may not catch it yet).

### ch07 — "Tamsin"  · N · 3,000w
- **Purpose:** The public turn; the alliance/use; introduce the journalist lens.
- **Beats:** Tamsin Okafor has been chasing a pattern — "rooms where everyone tells the truth," a string of strange collapses (the war-room leak, a confessing official). She triangulates to Daniel. First meeting: she tests him (lies in his presence, watches the field work on herself — she can't lie to him, unnerving and intimate). A deal: she'll keep him out of it / she'll tell it right. Both are using each other. Real spark of friendship under the transaction.
- **Close on:** Tamsin seeing the story of the decade; Daniel seeing a use, and, despite himself, a friend.

### ch08 — "Marcus"  · N · 3,400w
- **Purpose:** The condition's **purest good** — render with unironic uplift. The book earns its light here.
- **Beats:** Tamsin brings Daniel (quietly, before he's public) to a re-examination / innocence-project hearing for **Marcus Reyes**, 11 years wrongfully imprisoned. The recanting-then-recanting witness cannot sustain the lie in Daniel's presence; Daniel's silent suffering when *he* is asked something becomes legible proof of the field's reality. The lie breaks; the case breaks; Marcus walks free. Overwhelming, real good.
- **Close on:** Marcus in the daylight; Daniel feeling, for once, that the curse is a gift — the high the rest of the book will complicate.
- **Plant:** Marcus (returns in V); the courtroom-that-finally-works ideal.

### intC — "The Man Who Cannot Lie"  · I · 900w
- **Form:** Tamsin's *Lede* feature that breaks the story and **coins "the Believer"** (ironic kicker line: "He believes nothing. We are the believers now."). Journalistic, electric, slightly self-aggrandizing.
- **Purpose:** Launch the public era; name the myth.

### ch09 — "The Believer"  · N · 3,000w
- **Purpose:** The world arrives; first harms in miniature; loss of control over his own meaning.
- **Beats:** Post-publication. Crowds, cameras, the desperate, the litigious at his door. A first sour note: someone seeks him for a "reconciliation" that the forced truth *curdles* (an estranged parent says the true cruel thing). Daniel realizes he is now a public instrument and a private exile at once. June bullied/burdened at school for being "the Believer's kid."
- **Close on:** Daniel's home no longer his; the myth eating the man.

# PART III — THE PUBLIC SQUARE  (Months 4–7)

### ch10 — "Demand"  · N · 3,000w
- **Purpose:** Escalation; the contest over Daniel as asset; introduce Holm and Salt.
- **Beats:** Courts subpoena his presence; defense and prosecution fight over him; the desperate camp outside. **Renata Holm** (the Department) makes a measured first approach: the field is a national-security asset. **Victor Salt** (Halcyon) offers money/protection to study and "scale" it ("synthetic conviction"). Daniel, broke and unemployed and frightened, is courted by powers. Tamsin warns him.
- **Close on:** Daniel realizing he's no longer a person but a jurisdiction everyone wants to own.

### ch11 — "Good"  · N · 3,400w
- **Purpose:** **Good consequences peak** — three scenes, real grace, no irony (then a hairline of cost at the very end).
- **Beats:** Rendered as three linked scenes: (a) a **deathbed reconciliation** — a dying father and estranged son finally true, the merciful version of the condition; (b) a **corporate cover-up cracks** when execs can't sustain it in a deposition Daniel attends; (c) an **addict** who, in his presence, cannot lie himself into the next use — a small, fierce recovery. The condition as healer.
- **Close on:** genuine uplift — and one quiet image that suggests what such relentless truth might cost the people who can't bear it.
- **Balance note:** This chapter is the book's strongest *case for* the condition. Make it land.

### intD — "Welcome to the Quiet Room"  · I · 700w
- **Form:** Ad copy / brand brief for the new privacy economy: **"Honesty-Free Zones™,"** soundproof "Quiet Rooms," a dating app that pairs you *just outside the field*, noise-distance as luxury. Sharp consumer-capitalism satire of mediation as refuge.
- **Purpose:** Society routes around truth; the rooms-vs-screens theme commercialized.

### ch12 — "Bad"  · N · 3,200w
- **Purpose:** **Honesty as cruelty; the death of the white lie; the chilling effect** — the counterweight to ch11.
- **Beats:** A dinner party (friends, in his presence) detonates: true things that should never be said are said ("I never wanted the second kid," "I've always found you tedious"). The realization that cruelty needs no lies. People begin to *avoid* Daniel — social leprosy; the man no one can be near because no one can comfort or be comforted (no "you look great," no "it'll be okay"). A friend chooses, openly, never to see him again.
- **Close on:** Daniel alone at the center of an emptied room — the literal image of the field's isolation.

### ch13 — "What He Couldn't Take Back"  · N · 3,000w
- **Purpose:** The condition's first body. Handle the suicide off-page, with restraint; thematically load-bearing, never exploitative.
- **Beats:** A person (a public figure, or someone Daniel helped expose) is forced into a truth — a secret that, once spoken in his presence and out into the world, they cannot survive being known. Off-page death. Daniel's culpability question: he told no lie, compelled nothing — and a person is dead. Truth without mercy kills. Tamsin and Daniel reckon with their role.
- **Close on:** Daniel understanding that being unable to lie has not made him good; accuracy is not care.
- **Care note:** No method, no detail. The weight is moral, not graphic. (See style guide content rules.)

### ch14 — "Crane"  · N · 3,400w
- **Purpose:** The nemesis revealed — the loophole given a prophet. The book's pivot.
- **Beats:** **Elias Crane**, rising preacher, comes to Daniel — publicly, cameras rolling. Everyone expects the Believer to expose him. Instead Crane stands in the field and **feels nothing**, beaming, because he believes every word he says, including the false ones. He reframes Daniel on the spot: *"Pain is doubt leaving the body. He burns the liars and blesses the faithful. Believe, and you will never hurt again."* Daniel's power is *useless* against him — and worse, Crane has just turned the curse into a recruiting miracle.
- **Close on:** the crowd's awe; Daniel realizing he has met the one man he cannot touch — and that the man is going to be enormous.

### intE — "The Sermon at the Fairgrounds"  · I · 1,100w
- **Form:** Transcript of a Crane sermon — the full seductive doctrine of the Conviction in his own warm, dangerous voice. Genuinely moving in places; that's the point. Ends on the slogan and an altar call.
- **Purpose:** Let the antagonist's idea breathe at full strength so the reader feels its pull.

### ch15 — "The Summer of Tents"  · N · 3,000w
- **Purpose:** The self-deception epidemic; Daniel loses authorship of his own meaning.
- **Beats:** The Conviction spreads across the interior; stadiums of the painless faithful; "believe and you won't hurt" as mass practice. Daniel watches his condition become a creed he never authored and can't control. People train themselves into sincere delusion to escape the agony of doubt. He tries to speak against it — but his power can't touch belief, and the mediated crowd (rooms vs. screens) is beyond his field anyway.
- **Close on:** Daniel, the truth-bringer, watching truth lose — to comfort.

# PART IV — THE CREED  (Months 7–11)

### ch16 — "The Department"  · N · 3,000w
- **Purpose:** Weaponization begins; the state's claim.
- **Beats:** Renata Holm returns with leverage (legal jeopardy over his VMA past — possibly the *Priya/Halcombe* thread as the lever, tightening the noose toward his buried sin — or threat to custody of June). The argument, made by a patriot, not a sadist: a man who can make any prisoner unable to lie is too valuable to be free. Daniel is brought to the Site, "voluntarily."
- **Close on:** the unmarked door; the climate-sealed hum; the jurisdiction now pointed where the state wants.

### ch17 — "The Site"  · N · 3,400w
- **Purpose:** Truth as torture; the moral floor falls out.
- **Beats:** Daniel is positioned in interrogation rooms; detainees in his presence cannot lie — so they must betray or suffer in silence (the field as instrument; silence as the only, agonizing defense). He sees what his mere presence does. He tries to refuse, to leave the room, to warn; he's managed, compelled, complicit. A detainee he believes innocent. The banality of it — coffee cups, paperwork, a patriot's reasoning.
- **Close on:** Daniel realizing he has become the thing he most despises in a new key — not a liar now, but an instrument of others' cruelty, and *true.*

### intF — "MEMO — ASSET 'CONVICTION' — UTILITY ASSESSMENT"  · I · 800w
- **Form:** Redacted federal memo treating Daniel as ASSET; cost/benefit of the field as interrogation tool; euphemism, redaction bars, an appendix on "containment of subject's family." Bureaucratic horror, dead-cold.
- **Purpose:** The state's view, in its own bloodless prose; mirrors intA (corporate) — both institutions reduce a man to a line item.

### ch18 — "What Comfort Costs"  · N · 3,200w
- **Purpose:** Total isolation; the marriage's collapse; June and the loophole — the book's most intimate devastation.
- **Beats:** Released/returned, hollowed. The marriage cannot survive a love with no mercy in it — Daniel literally cannot say "it's going to be okay," cannot spare Nadia anything; she can't be comforted by a man who can't lie and won't stay silent kindly. June, to survive the no-lie house, has **learned the loophole** — she's teaching herself to *believe* her own comforts, a nine-year-old becoming serenely, protectively self-deceived. Daniel sees his daughter choosing delusion to be safe, and understands the whole world is doing the same.
- **Close on:** June, dry-eyed, telling herself something untrue and feeling no pain; Daniel unable to correct her because the correction would only hurt.
- **Pay/plant:** June's loophole (planted earlier) lands; Nadia thesis pays toward the marriage's end.

### intG — "thread: how to beat the believer (it actually works)"  · I · 700w
- **Form:** A message-board / forum thread: the loophole as folk wisdom ("just believe it lol," self-hypnosis tips, "doubters cope"), the hardening of **"doubter"** into a slur, the marketplace of belief-coaching. Crowd-sourced self-deception.
- **Purpose:** The epidemic at street level; "doubter" becomes a weapon.

### ch19 — "Doubters"  · N · 3,200w
- **Purpose:** The world inverted — the honest persecuted; national nadir.
- **Beats:** The Conviction goes political (Crane a candidate/force). The honest — those who still suffer because they still know — are recast as "doubters," "secret liars who won't believe hard enough." A scrupulous public figure is destroyed precisely *for* visibly suffering near a Believer-adjacent test (i.e., for being honest). Truth-telling becomes the mark of the damned. **Father Anton** appears: the Jesuit who names the condition as a curse-without-grace, the one religious reading that isn't Crane's.
- **Close on:** Daniel grasping the full catastrophe: his curse, meant to enforce truth, has built a machine for mass, sincere, *painless* lying — and made honesty a sin.

### intH — "Set List"  · I · 700w
- **Form:** A stand-up comedy set / club transcript built on the fact that **jokes don't hurt** (art's exemption) — until a heckler weaponizes it and the room turns; the exemption itself becomes suspect ("if it didn't hurt, did you mean it?"). Funny, then unsettling.
- **Purpose:** Art's exemption (style guide edge case) dramatized; collapsing trust in the one sanctioned untruth.

### ch20 — "The Refusal"  · N · 3,400w
- **Purpose:** The hinge of Daniel's redemption — the ex-liar chooses pain over deceit; personal nadir.
- **Beats:** Pulled back to the Site (or a final deployment), ordered to be present while an innocent is broken. Daniel does the one thing the curse has made unthinkable: to sabotage it / protect the prisoner, he attempts a **structural lie** — and it nearly kills him (seizure, burst vessels; calibrate to *structural*). The man who lied for a living now bleeds rather than let truth be used as a weapon. It costs catastrophically; it may not even work. Released, broken in body. The marriage ends, clear-eyed, here or carried into V.
- **Close on:** Daniel on the floor, mouth full of blood, having chosen — for the first time in his life — to lie *for* someone instead of against them. Rock bottom and turning point in one image.

# PART V — RECKONING  (Months 11–14)

### ch21 — "Priya"  · N · 3,200w
- **Purpose:** The buried truth resurfaces; state the final, unsolvable problem.
- **Beats:** **Priya Raman** — alive — comes back into Daniel's orbit (Holm's leverage exposed it, or Tamsin found her, or she sought him). The eleven dead, the falsified test, the campaign that destroyed her. The horror: Daniel *cannot be compelled to confess it,* because he has genuinely believed his own version — the belief loophole protects the one lie that matters. The curse that exposes everyone cannot reach his deepest deceit. **The book's final problem: the one truth that matters can't be extracted; it has to be chosen.**
- **Close on:** Daniel face to face with the woman he erased, feeling no pain, and understanding that *that* is the most damning thing about him.

### ch22 — "Grace"  · N · 3,000w
- **Purpose:** The humane counterweight; Daniel decides; Marcus's verdict.
- **Beats:** **Father Anton:** truth without mercy is the opposite of grace; a curse, not a gift; what would it mean to choose the truth the curse can't force? **Marcus Reyes** returns to deliver the plainest verdict on what the condition is worth — neither Crane's nor Holm's nor Salt's; a freed man's hard, generous, unsentimental accounting (freedom isn't wholeness; truth gave him his life back and nothing else; and still he'd take it). Daniel resolves to break his own belief about Priya — in public, where it will cost everything — because no one can make him, which is the only reason it would mean anything.
- **Close on:** Daniel choosing the confrontation; the decision made.

### intI — "NTSB Factual Report — Excerpt"  · I · 800w
- **Form:** Cold aviation-report / recall officialese on the Halcombe flight: the falsified test, the part, the **eleven** names in an appendix. The buried truth in the public record at last, bloodless and total.
- **Purpose:** Let "eleven" finally be true on the page before Daniel makes it true with his mouth.

### ch23 — "The Confrontation"  · N · 3,600w
- **Purpose:** Climax-as-confession; power useless, honesty everything.
- **Beats:** Public, mediated-but-present showdown with **Crane** (note the field/mediation irony: a stadium where the field can't reach the masses, but Crane and Daniel share one stage, one radius). Daniel's power is useless against Crane's sincerity — Crane lies serenely, painlessly, and the crowd loves him. Daniel cannot beat the prophet by exposing him. So he does the only thing left: he breaks **his own** most necessary belief — confesses Priya, the falsified test, the **eleven**, his own erasure of a good woman — out loud, in the field, at maximal, body-shattering cost (he must overcome his own self-deception to *know* it's true, which makes it hurt; the pain is the proof he means it). The confession does what exposure couldn't: it shows the crowd what truth actually costs, and what belief-without-cost is worth. Crane is wounded — not destroyed.
- **Close on:** Daniel, bleeding, having told the one truth no curse could compel; the crowd's silence; Crane's serene, undefeated smile beginning, for the first time, to flicker.
- **Pay:** Priya, eleven, the loophole, Gideon's creed inverted, phones/mediation irony.

### ch24 — "Aftermath"  · N · 2,800w
- **Purpose:** No reset; tally without verdict; earned ambiguity.
- **Beats:** The world after. The condition persists (or its status is left deliberately uncertain — do **not** cure it cleanly). Crane wounded but his movement endures (sincere conviction outlives its prophet's flicker). Holm's program quietly continues. Salt's "synthetic conviction" ships anyway. The good and the bad both persist, neither winning. Legal/personal consequences of Daniel's confession (Halcombe, his ruin, perhaps charges). The marriage settles into an honest, separate, clear-eyed peace — not reconciliation, something truer and sadder.
- **Close on:** the world unchanged in its division, changed forever in its weather.

### ch25 — "The Edge of the Room"  · N · 2,600w
- **Purpose:** Final image; the book's question left open; small real connection survives.
- **Beats:** A quiet place after the noise. A small, true human connection holds — **June** (the catechism pays off: she asks the one question, and Daniel answers it truly, and for once the truth is also a mercy — or it isn't, and that's the book). Maybe Nadia in a new honest key; maybe Marcus or Priya in grace. The condition still on, the world still split between the honest who suffer and the convinced who don't. End on an **image, not a verdict**, that leaves genuinely open whether a world of compelled truth is better or worse. Last line resonant, earned, quiet.
- **Pay:** June's catechism; the edge-of-the-room motif as title of the final chapter; the whole book's ambivalence held in one closing image.

## Drafting reminders (every unit)
- One book, one voice — obey [../wiki/06-style-guide.md](../wiki/06-style-guide.md).
- Calibrate pain to lie-size every time ([../wiki/07-canon-and-glossary.md](../wiki/07-canon-and-glossary.md) table).
- Never explain the condition in narration. Discover it through bodies.
- Keep BOTH poles (good/bad) alive; never let the book settle the argument.
- Land openings in scene/image; land endings on a turn or resonant line.
- Plant and pay off per the ledger. Don't contradict canon.
