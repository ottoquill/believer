**SLIDE 2 — The VMA Method (for incoming associates)**

We do not deny. A denial is a structure the camera loves to photograph, and it photographs as guilt.

We **reframe.** The facts are timber; we follow the grain already in the wood. Nothing is fabricated. Everything is composed.

| Don't say | Do say |
|---|---|
| "Layoffs" | "Right-sizing for resilience" |
| "Chemical spill" | "A community comes together" |
| "We lost the data" | "Committed to transparency going forward" |
| "No comment" | "We welcome any legitimate review" |

Study the last one. Audit the verb *welcome* and the modifier *legitimate*: together they concede nothing, perform everything, and dissolve before anyone can parse them. A sentence that sounds like surrender and functions as a moat. Bill it as senior strategy, because it is.

**SLIDE 3 — Managed Disclosure: The Three Beats**

Every problem is a story with a beginning we author, a middle we compress, and an end somewhere else.

1. **Pre-bunk.** Plant the counter-narrative before the accusation lands. An audience will revise a story it already holds; it will rarely abandon one. So get there first, and get there *bored.*
2. **Genre shift.** Never argue the allegation on its merits. Argue the *category* of the conversation. "Rumor," not "report." "Timing," not "substance." Remind the serious third of the room that they once meant to cover something that mattered.
3. **Relocation.** Move the cycle elsewhere by supplying a better sentence elsewhere — sharper, shorter, more repeatable. Repeatability outperforms accuracy in every measured cycle. The audience is not unintelligent. The audience is occupied.

KPI: *time-to-elsewhere.* Target under 72 hours.

**SLIDE 6 — Containment Protocols: The Aggrieved Individual (Doctrine)**

We do not attack the claim. Claims invite discovery, and claims have a habit of turning out true.

We address the *credibility of the claimant* — never by invention, always by **fuller context.** Surface the history. Let the audience meet who the source *is* before they hear what the source *says.* A witness, sufficiently introduced, requires no rebuttal.

The grievance reads as a symptom. The whistle reads as a tell.

Recommended frame: *unwell, not dishonest.* Generous on its face. Unfalsifiable in practice. Adhesive.
