## Memo: Asset 'Conviction' - Utility Assessment

*Department interagency memorandum — classification: TS//[REDACTED]//NOFORN — distribution limited to read-in personnel*

**MEMORANDUM FOR THE RECORD**

**SUBJECT:** Utility Assessment — Asset designation CONVICTION (the "field")
**PREPARED BY:** Holm, R. — Program Coordination
**RE:** Continued integration of CONVICTION into custodial-interview operations at the Site

---

**1. SUMMARY.** This memo assesses the operational value of Asset CONVICTION ("the Asset," ref. subject M█████, age 44, formerly of ████████████████████) against the costs of continued retention. The assessment is favorable. The Asset is recommended for continued integration on a managed basis. Concerns raised last quarter regarding the Asset's cooperation are addressed at §4.

**2. CAPABILITY (as observed; mechanism not established).** Within a defined radius of the Asset's physical person — measured at the Site as approximately 9–10 meters, soft-edged — subjects experience an involuntary physiological deterrent against the spoken assertion of statements the subject knows to be untrue. Severity scales with the magnitude of the untruth. Subjects retain the capacity to (a) remain silent and (b) assert untruths they sincerely believe. The deterrent does not compel disclosure. It raises the cost of denial.

Operationally, this resolves the central inefficiency of the prior toolkit. Conventional methods produce statements; they do not produce statements of known reliability. CONVICTION produces a clean instrument: the subject who will not betray an associate is left only silence or suffering, and silence in proximity to the Asset is itself legible. The placement of a silence — its timing, its location within a sequence — has in ████ of ████ reviewed sessions been assessed as actionable.

**3. COST-BENEFIT.**

| Factor | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Reliability of product | High. Self-validating. No corroboration overhead. |
| Deniability | High. No marks, no method, nothing on the subject's body. The instrument is the room. |
| Throughput | Moderate. Single-Asset; non-replicable to date (cf. Halcyon "synthetic conviction" R&D, not yet viable). |
| Channel limitation | Capability does not transmit by telephone, recording, video link, or written word. Subjects must be co-located with the Asset in person. The instrument cannot be networked. |
| Reputational exposure | Material. See §4 and Appendix B. |
| Asset stability | See §4. |

The channel limitation merits emphasis. We hold an instrument that works only in rooms, in a century that has otherwise moved out of them. Every other tool the Department fields reaches across the world at the speed of light and returns product of uncertain truth value. This one returns reliable product and cannot leave the room. We assess this as a feature for our purposes and a comfort for our adversaries', who need only keep their distance — as, increasingly, they do.

**4. ASSET COOPERATION AND STABILITY.** The Asset has on ███ occasions attempted to remove himself from interview rooms, to delay or degrade sessions, and on one occasion to warn a subject of the capability prior to questioning (the warning was, by the nature of the capability, of limited use). The Asset has characterized his role in language the program need not adopt. These are management items, not disqualifiers. The Asset's leverage profile remains favorable: ongoing exposure relating to prior commercial activity (ref. ████████ Aerospace; the engineer matter; the ██ fatalities) provides durable inducement, and the Asset's continued belief that those events were unrelated is, for our purposes, immaterial — what matters is that the record can be made to matter. Custody and welfare considerations regarding dependents are addressed at Appendix B and provide additional, lower-cost inducement should §4-class incidents recur.

A note for the record, raised by the preparer and overruled at review: the Asset does not lie. The instrument we hold is a man who, by his nature, cannot deceive the people in front of him, deployed by an agency whose entire architecture rests on the people in front of it not knowing what is true. The contradiction has been noted and filed. It does not affect the recommendation.

**5. RECOMMENDATION.** Continue integration. Maintain leverage. Resolve §4 incidents by the least disruptive available means. Do not, under any circumstances, permit the Asset to be questioned, by anyone, in his own presence, regarding the program.

Given a sufficient reason and enough time, a subject will reliably arrange to stop knowing the thing that hurts, and we are in the business of providing reasons. A nine-year-old has demonstrated the principle at no cost to the program. We need only not interrupt her.
