Same questions keep coming, so. If you've ever been hauled into a deposition or an HR room with one of *them* sitting across from you to "verify," and panicked and bled onto the table — this is for you. You don't have to bleed. Four months now, not a wince.

The mistake everyone makes is thinking the goal is to *not say the thing.* Wrong. Silence reads. They watch where you go quiet and the quiet is the confession — ask anyone whose marriage ended on a "no comment." The goal is to make the thing true *for you.* You're not lying if you believe it. That's the only rule there is.

So: pick the thing you need true. Say it out loud, alone, twenty times a day. Not "I'm telling myself it's true" — that's the trap, that's doubt in a hat, and it still hurts. You have to get under the part of you keeping score. Tips below. 🔥
