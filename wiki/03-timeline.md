# 03 — Timeline

Spans roughly **fourteen months**, present day. Seasons matter (the Christmas chapter; a summer of revival tents; an autumn election). Keep the chronology consistent; the dossier interludes can range freely in time but should be dated relative to the onset.

## Pre-onset (backstory, delivered in fragments)
- **~5 years prior:** Daniel makes partner at Vance Mercer Albright. Marries Nadia eleven years ago; June born.
- **~14 months before onset:** Halcombe Aerospace hires the firm. Priya Raman's safety report surfaces.
- **~10 months before onset:** Daniel runs the campaign that destroys Priya. The story dies.
- **~4 months before onset:** Halcombe regional jet, Flight crash, eleven dead. Daniel "reframes" it as pilot error / unrelated. Believes it. (Buried, not gone.)
- **Day −1:** The firm's biggest win — Daniel saves a client (a governor) with a masterstroke of spin at a televised press conference. Champagne. The novel's first scene: a virtuoso of lying at the peak of his powers.

## Part I — Onset ("The Wince") — Days 1–~21
- **Day 1, morning:** Onset. Daniel's first reflexive lie of the day lances him in his own kitchen. In the same room, Nadia, asked an ordinary question, answers with a truth she never meant to say. Neither understands yet.
- **Days 1–7:** Private terror. Daniel runs experiments (alone, in mirrors, on the phone vs. in person) and **maps Rules 1 & 2.** Discovers the field via colleagues and strangers buckling around him.
- **Days 7–14:** Career implosion. A client meeting becomes a massacre of involuntary candor. Gideon Vance realizes Daniel is radioactive. Daniel is eased out / suspended.
- **Days 14–21:** Domestic reckoning accelerates. The marriage cannot hold its necessary fictions. June begins to notice. End of Part I: Daniel accepts the condition is permanent and total.

## Part II — Proximity — ~Weeks 4–12
- Daniel withdraws, experiments further, and **discovers the belief loophole** (devastating scene: a confident liar near him feels nothing; a scrupulous honest man suffers). Horror, not relief.
- Tamsin Okafor tracks the strange pattern of "rooms where everyone tells the truth." She finds Daniel.
- **First public good:** the **Marcus Reyes** re-examination. Triumph.
- Tamsin's piece runs; the epithet **"the Believer"** is coined. The world begins to arrive at Daniel's door.
- First *harms* surface in miniature (a reconciliation that curdles; a forced truth that wounds).

## Part III — The Public Square — ~Months 4–7
- Demand explodes: courts, the state (Renata Holm's first approach), Victor Salt's Halcyon, activists, the desperate.
- **Good consequences peak:** an exoneration cascade; a corporate cover-up cracks; a dying father and son reconcile in his presence; a Truth-and-Reconciliation pilot.
- **Bad consequences deepen:** honesty as cruelty (the dinner-party catastrophe; the suicide after a forced truth); the chilling effect (people flee his presence; social leprosy); the death of the white lie and what it does to love.
- **Crane emerges.** He visits Daniel publicly, feels no pain, and is *transfigured* by it. Founds the **Conviction.** Summer of tent revivals. The self-deception epidemic begins.

## Part IV — The Creed / Black Site (descent) — ~Months 7–11
- **Weaponization:** Renata Holm brings Daniel to a federal site. The field as interrogation instrument — truth as torture (you cannot lie, so you betray or suffer in silence). Daniel's complicity; his attempt to refuse; the moral floor falls out.
- **Isolation:** the marriage fractures (no mercy possible); June and the loophole; Daniel radioactive, abandoned, a man no one can bear to be near because no one can comfort him or be comforted.
- **Crane ascends:** the Conviction becomes a political movement; painless fanatics fill stadiums; the honest are scapegoated as "doubters" and "liars who haven't believed hard enough." The curse meant to enforce truth has birthed mass delusion. National nadir + personal nadir converge. A death (an ally or a near one) or an irreversible betrayal.

## Part V — Reckoning (earned ambiguity) — ~Months 11–14
- Priya Raman resurfaces — alive. Daniel's buried lie is forced toward the surface, but the *belief loophole protects it:* he cannot be compelled to confess what he's convinced himself didn't happen. The book's final problem: **the one truth that matters can't be extracted; it has to be chosen.**
- **The confrontation with Crane** — public, televised-but-also-present (note the field/mediation irony): Daniel's power is useless against Crane's sincerity. Daniel wins, if he wins, only by doing the thing the curse never could make him do — breaking his own most necessary belief, in public, at maximal cost, telling the truth about Priya and the eleven dead and himself.
- **Aftermath:** No reset. The condition persists (or its status is left deliberately uncertain). The world is permanently altered. Crane is wounded but not cleanly defeated — sincere conviction endures. The marriage settles into honest separateness. A small, real human connection survives. The book ends on an image, not a verdict, that leaves open whether a world of compelled truth is a better or worse place. **Show, don't rule.**
