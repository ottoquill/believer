# 04 — Places & Organizations

Geography is deliberately semi-anonymous (a heightened-real America). Name institutions; keep the city unnamed ("the city," "downtown," "the harbor district"). Washington is named. The heartland is "the interior."

## Places
- **The city** — large, coastal, moneyed and unequal; glass towers downtown, a working harbor, brownstone neighborhoods. Daniel's world. Unnamed throughout.
- **The Mercer apartment** — high floor, glass, a kitchen with an island where the marriage breaks on Day 1. Recurs as the barometer of the family.
- **Vance Mercer Albright (VMA)** — the firm. A floor of hushed carpet, no-window war rooms, a "situation room" where narratives are built. The temple of the managed lie.
- **The Lede** — Tamsin's outlet. A serious newsroom on hard times; cramped, fluorescent, idealistic, broke.
- **The courthouse / the Reyes re-examination** — Part II/III high point. A plain hearing room transformed into the most honest space in the country simply because Daniel sits in the gallery.
- **The Site** — a federal facility (Part IV). Unmarked, climate-sealed, fluorescent. Where the field becomes an instrument. Renders the banality of state truth-extraction.
- **The Conviction grounds** — Crane's revival circuit: stadium tents in the interior, then arenas, then a movement headquarters. Sawdust, jumbotrons, the smell of fried dough and certainty.
- **The retreat / the end** — a quiet coastal or rural place where the final, ambiguous scene lands. Chosen for stillness after the noise.

## Organizations & forces
- **Vance Mercer Albright** — crisis comms / reputation management. Motto-in-spirit: *"There are no facts, only the story nobody's beaten yet."* The pre-onset moral universe.
- **Halcombe Aerospace** — former client; the falsified safety test; Flight (eleven dead); Priya Raman's employer. The buried sin.
- **Halcyon** — Victor Salt's tech conglomerate. Wants to study/replicate/contain the field. Funds "**synthetic conviction**" research (belief-on-demand; the satire of engineered self-deception). Techno-solutionism as the secular mirror of Crane's faith.
- **The Department / Renata Holm's office** — federal; sees the field as a strategic asset. The black-site arc. Argues utility over liberty.
- **The Conviction** — Elias Crane's movement. Doctrine: pain = doubt leaving the body; *believe and you will not hurt.* Recasts Daniel as a divine instrument that "burns liars, blesses the faithful." Grows from tent revival → media phenomenon → political machine. The book's central antagonistic institution and its most seductive idea.
- **The chorus / the feed** — not an org but a presence: cable news, podcasts, message boards, ad agencies. The realm the field can never reach (mediated, distant) — the free country of lies that thrives in contrast to every room Daniel enters. The dossier interludes live here.

## Recurring physical motifs of place
- **Rooms vs. screens.** Truth is what happens in rooms; lies are what survive on screens. Stage every key scene aware of which side of that line it's on.
- **The edge of the room.** People unconsciously gravitate to the soft boundary of the field. A room with Daniel in it has a strange empty center and a crowded perimeter.
- **Sound-proofing, glass, distance.** Architecture becomes a way to manage truth. Powerful people build setbacks. The phone becomes sacred — the last private place to lie.
