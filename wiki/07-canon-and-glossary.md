# 07 — Canon & Glossary (continuity ledger)

Do-not-contradict facts, named terms, and a running ledger. Amend deliberately.

## Named terms / in-world vocabulary
- **The condition / the affliction** — neutral terms for what Daniel has.
- **The field** — the proximity effect (Rule 2). Daniel privately calls it "the room."
- **"the Believer"** — the public epithet, coined by Tamsin Okafor in her first *Lede* piece (Part II). Ironic: Daniel believes nothing. Becomes the movement's name-seed.
- **The Conviction** — Elias Crane's movement and doctrine. Members are "the Convicted" / "the faithful." Core slogan: **"Believe, and you will not hurt."** Also: *"Pain is doubt leaving the body."* and *"He burns the liars and blesses the faithful."*
- **The Doubt / doubters** — Crane's term for the honest who still suffer (i.e., the unconverted). A slur by Part IV.
- **Synthetic conviction** — Victor Salt / Halcyon's R&D program: engineered belief-on-demand. The secular mirror of the Conviction.
- **Honesty-Free Zone™** — commercial product (dossier satire): shielded spaces (distance/soundproofing) marketed as places you can still lie. Status symbol.
- **The wince** — colloquial, the visible tell of someone fighting the field.

## Pain calibration table (keep consistent)
| Lie size | Example | Pain signature |
|---|---|---|
| Trivial | "I'm fine." "Nice to meet you." | sting, wince, foil-taste, flashbulb behind eyes |
| Moderate | a covered small betrayal, a flattering fib with stakes | headache surge, sweat, dizziness, dry-mouth |
| Consequential | a hidden affair, a falsified figure, ruining someone | migraine, nosebleed, knees buckle, battery-taste, near-blackout |
| Structural | market-moving / prison-sending / war-starting lie | seizure, burst vessels, collapse; potentially fatal if sustained |
